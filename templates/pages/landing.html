Weather-aware care

Generic schedules, turned into smart local ones

Greenhand checks the forecast for your location every few hours and adjusts your reminders. You can override any change.

Rain rule

5 mm of rain in the last 24h, or a 70% chance in the next 12h, skips outdoor watering. Indoor plants are never affected.

Frost rule

Lows at or below 2 °C within 48h trigger an alert for frost-sensitive outdoor plants — with what to do about it.

Heat rule Coming soon

Heat waves of 32 °C and up suggest extra watering and shade.

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Ask the tutor — it’s what it’s there for.

Do I need a backyard?

No. Greenhand supports backyards, balconies, windowsills, and indoor spaces, and recommends plants that suit the space you have.

How does the tutor know about my garden?

Each message includes your profile, your plants and their stages, your last 14 days of logs, open problems, and your local forecast. Answers are grounded in a curated plant-care knowledge base and cite sources where possible.

What if the AI gets a diagnosis wrong?

Every diagnosis shows a confidence level and signs to watch for, and sets a follow-up reminder. All AI content is labeled, and for serious issues the tutor points you to your local extension office.

Is my location private?

Location is stored at city or ZIP precision by default, GPS data is stripped from uploaded photos, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Greenhand is a mobile-first web app, built for snapping photos right in the garden.
