How your answer is made

  1. Your question is checked and cleaned (length, empty input, injection attempts, daily limit).
  2. Your plant’s stage, recent logs and the {{ gh_location.label }} forecast are added.
  3. The closest sections of Greenhand’s plant-care guides are found by meaning, not keywords.
  4. An open AI model running on our own server writes the answer and cites the guides it used.
