# Watering basics

## When to water
Check the soil before you water instead of following the calendar blindly. Push a finger 2–3 cm into the soil: if it is dry at that depth, water; if it is still moist, wait a day and check again. Containers dry out much faster than garden beds, especially small pots, terracotta pots and pots on a sunny or windy balcony. In hot weather (above 30 °C) small pots may need water every day.

## How to water
Water deeply and slowly at the base of the plant until water runs out of the drainage holes, then let the excess drain away. Deep watering grows deep roots; frequent small sips keep roots shallow. Water in the morning so leaves dry during the day, and avoid wetting the leaves, which spreads fungal diseases such as powdery mildew and blight. Never leave a pot standing in a saucer of water.

## Signs of overwatering
Overwatering is the most common beginner mistake. Signs: soft, yellow lower leaves; wilting even though the soil is wet; a sour smell from the soil; fungus gnats flying around the pot; dark, mushy stems at the soil line (root rot). Fix: stop watering until the top 2–3 cm of soil is dry, make sure the pot has drainage holes and drains freely, remove yellow leaves, and empty the saucer. If the roots are brown and mushy, repot into fresh dry potting mix and cut off rotten roots.

## Signs of underwatering
Leaves droop and feel dry or crispy at the edges, the soil pulls away from the sides of the pot, and the pot feels light when lifted. Fix: water slowly until water drains out the bottom. If the soil has become so dry that water runs straight through, stand the pot in a bowl of water for 20–30 minutes so it soaks up from below.

## Rain and watering
After 5 mm or more of rain in the last 24 hours, outdoor plants in the ground or in open containers usually do not need watering that day. Greenhand skips outdoor watering reminders automatically after rain. Plants under a roof overhang or on a covered balcony may stay dry even when it rains, so still check their soil. Indoor plants are never affected by rain.
